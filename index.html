Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Termite (also known as Termite Habit Tracker, package io.github.termite) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is stored locally on your device, what pseudonymous technical and purchase data is processed by third-party SDKs, and your privacy rights under applicable laws.

1. Information We Collect & How Storage Works

1.1 Local-First Storage on Your Device

Termite is designed as an offline-first habit tracker. All habits, daily check-ins, timer sessions, statistics, and application preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's internal Room (SQLite) database and DataStore preference files.

  • No User Accounts & No Developer Cloud Servers: Termite does not require account registration or sign-in, and we do not maintain developer-operated cloud servers that sync or store your habit entries.
  • Manual JSON Backups: If you use the Pro backup feature to export or import a JSON backup file, the file is generated locally and handled via Android's system file picker or share sheet. You control where exported backup files are saved or shared.
  • Android System Auto Backup: Termite enables standard Android Auto Backup in its application manifest so your local database and preferences can be preserved across device transfers or reinstalls if you have Google device backup enabled in your Android settings. These backups are managed by Android and your personal Google account settings, not by developer servers.

1.2 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share:

  • Direct personal contact details (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address) inside the App.
  • The specific habit names, check-in records, notes, or schedules stored in your local database.
  • Precise or approximate GPS location, contacts, camera/microphone recordings, or biometric data.

1.3 Data Processed by Third-Party Services

To support in-app purchases, crash diagnostics, basic app analytics, and in-app ratings, Termite integrates the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Data Processed
Google Play Billing & In-App Review Payment processing for subscriptions and lifetime upgrades, plus native in-app rating prompts Purchase status, order/transaction verification, and Play Store rating availability handled by Google Play Services (we never see your credit card or payment details)
RevenueCat Subscription and Pro entitlement validation across plans (Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime, Free Trial) Pseudonymous RevenueCat App User ID, purchase receipts/tokens, and active entitlement status
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reporting and app stability diagnostics Crash traces, stack dumps, device model, Android OS version, app version, and a pseudonymous Crashlytics installation ID
Firebase Analytics Aggregated app usage, session, and stability telemetry (with Advertising ID collection disabled) Pseudonymous Firebase app-instance ID, basic app session/lifecycle events, device model, OS version, and general region/language metadata

2. Permissions We Request

Termite requests only the Android permissions needed for its core features and SDK operations:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to display scheduled habit reminders and timer completion alerts.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger time-sensitive habit reminders and focus timer alerts at your chosen times.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Allows the App to reschedule your habit reminders and refresh home screen widgets after a device restart, timezone/time change, or app update.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to verify purchases via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, and to transmit pseudonymous Crashlytics and Analytics diagnostics.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): Enables in-app subscriptions and one-time Lifetime purchases through Google Play.
  • Background Execution (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Included via AndroidX WorkManager for reliable background widget refreshes and daily habit status updates.

3. Advertising & Advertising ID

No Third-Party Ads: Termite displays no third-party advertisements and does not integrate AdMob or any advertising SDK. We explicitly remove the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission from the merged application manifest and disable Advertising ID collection in Firebase Analytics.

4. Diagnostics, Analytics & Purchase Data

Diagnostic logs from Firebase Crashlytics, session telemetry from Firebase Analytics, and entitlement records from RevenueCat use pseudonymous installation or app-instance identifiers rather than your real-world identity. We use this information solely to fix crashes, maintain app reliability, and unlock your purchased Termite PRO entitlements.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

You can delete all locally stored habit data at any time by clearing App storage via Android Settings > Apps > Termite > Storage & cache > Clear storage or by uninstalling the App. If Android system Auto Backup is enabled on your device, you can manage or delete system backups in your Google Drive / Android backup settings.

Retention & Support Requests: Termite does not offer user accounts. Pseudonymous crash/analytics telemetry is retained by Firebase according to standard diagnostic retention windows, and Google Play / RevenueCat retain transaction records as needed to restore active entitlements and comply with statutory tax and financial recordkeeping laws. For privacy inquiries or requests concerning pseudonymous records, contact sumitkahar073@gmail.com. Verified requests are processed promptly and within 30 days.

6. Data Security

Your local habit database and preferences are protected by Android's application sandbox and device-level encryption. Network communications with Google Play, RevenueCat, and Firebase are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Please protect your device with a strong screen lock (PIN, password, or biometric lock).

7. Children's Privacy

Termite is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or the applicable digital age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact sumitkahar073@gmail.com so we can promptly delete it.
